Diagnostics (Opus, from the run):
- **Stationary user is not detected.** 286–345 s: cadence is reported throughout (1.3–2.2 Hz, repeated held 1.50, one **−16.3 Hz** at 336 s), so ego speed stays ~1.1 m/s. Bob RMS (dy, 4 s window) is 0.0019–0.0030 at 290–304 s, the same range as walking at 36 s (0.0029); it falls to 0.0005–0.0015 only after 326 s. Bob amplitude alone does not separate standing from walking.
- **Motion state of movers** is `closing − ego_speed`. When cadence is null the ego speed falls back to the pole-witness speed × k (0.59 at 178.7 s while walking ~1.4), so a static person reads "approaching".
- **Collision course** for movers looks at lateral offset now, not the mover's own lateral velocity, so a vehicle driving across the crosswalk is a danger and drives the beeps.
- **Surface evidence:** a curb running along the path near the feet satisfies `note_evidence` (bottom rows, in lane, width ≥ 0.15) without being crossed.

## Decisions (Opus, to confirm with the user on the next review)
1. **Motion state is spoken for people and riders**, from their own ground velocity (world frame: closing − ego speed along the line of sight, lateral from the lateral-offset derivative corrected for our heading change):
   - static (|v| < `stationaryMps`, with the existing hysteresis): `"{label} standing at {clock} in {steps}"` (riders: `"{label} stopped at …"`);
   - approaching on a collision course: `"{label} approaching at {clock}"`, **no distance** (user: speed makes it meaningless);
   - moving away inside the walking lane: `"{label} moving away at {clock}"`, no distance;
   - motion not measured yet / ego speed unknown: today's `"{label} at {clock} in {steps}"`.
   - Templates in `collision.json` `speech.templates` (`standing`, `stopped`, `approach`, `receding`).
2. **Movers outside a collision course are silent and do not beep.** Predict the mover's lateral position at the time we would reach it (or closest approach, for movers with their own velocity): collision course iff |predicted lateral| ≤ `movingCollisionHalfWidthM` and the meeting is within `movingDangerSteps`. A vehicle or person crossing ahead that will have cleared the lane is not a danger. Static people beside the path keep today's rule (within `movingLaneHalfWidthM`, spoken with "standing").
3. **Vehicles are never context.** Remove `vehicle` and `parked car` from `contextSpeak`. A parked car in the lane stays a static danger (collision course by definition).
4. **Curbs once per crossing.** `featureLabelOverrides.curb` like the crosswalk (gap ~20 s, newCrossingSteps ~14), and a curb is not announced when a crosswalk was announced < 20 s ago and lies within its steps (the cane picks up the curb at the crossing; the user wants "curb and poles" once, before the crossing). Check against 39.0 s → expected phrase in that slot: curb + poles (thin notice), then nothing at 40.5 and 46.2.
5. **Surface change needs a curb/crosswalk that crossed the lane.** `note_evidence` only for a track that held role `feature` (lane-crossing, not `along_path`) in at least `confirmFrames` frames before going under. Expected: no "road" at 159.8, and so no "sidewalk" at 244.
6. **Stationary user.** New ego state `stationary` (hazards `ego.stationary`). While stationary:
   - nothing static is announced (context, feature, static danger, thin notice), and no final/beeps for static items;
   - people/riders/vehicles only when **approaching on a collision course** (their own velocity, since ours is 0).
   - Detection is an **investigation task** for the worker: bob amplitude does not separate the cases (see above). Candidates, in order: (a) visual forward motion: expansion/scale of the lower-frame image between motion frames (analysis-side, 160×90, adds `motion.npz` field `zoom`; requires re-analysis, fine for the test clip), (b) static-witness closing speeds near 0 from depth history (DA2 per-item depth is in every run), (c) cadence spectral quality (peak ratio, harmonic consistency) with a stricter threshold. Pick the one that separates 286–345 s (static, per the user) from 0–280 s (walking) on 4929 with margin; report the numbers. Enter stationary after ~1.5 s, leave after ~1 s of walking evidence.
7. **Ego speed when cadence drops:** hold the last cadence-based speed (up to `cadenceWindowSeconds`) instead of the witness fallback; then unknown → people get no motion word.
8. **Cadence refine bug:** reject a parabolic refine outside the search band / outside ±1 bin (−16.3 Hz at 336 s).
9. **TTS "tarmac":** `audio.js` sets `u.lang = "en-US"` and picks an English voice from `speechSynthesis.getVoices()` (cached, `voiceschanged`); the browser default voice is probably not English. The user verifies by ear.
