## Decisions
1. **People (person, animal):**
   - **Phrase window:** spoken once at **5–10 steps**, always **with distance** and the state word, if measured: `"person approaching at 12:15 in 9"`, `"person moving away at 12:30 in 8"`, `"person standing at …"`, or plain `"person at …"`.
   - **Which people:** anyone within the people lateral zone counts, whatever their direction (the user wants to know someone is there). The zone is `peopleSpeakLateralM`, ~2.5 m; check it against the ⊘ at 3.0 m and the + at 2.4 m.
   - **Under 5 steps:** no speech for people, beeps only (if on a collision course). The template `approach` for people gets the distance back.
2. **Fast movers (rider/cyclist, vehicle) approaching on or near a collision course:**
   - **When:** announced **as early as possible**, with no 10-step cap. Trigger on time-to-meet (e.g. distance / closing ≤ `fastWarnSeconds` ≈ 6 s) or as soon as the approach is confirmed, up to maxRange.
   - **Wording:** no distance (`"cyclist approaching at 11"`).
   - **Near course:** predicted lateral at meeting ≤ `movingLaneHalfWidthM`.
   - **Investigation:** why 445.3's cyclist (track 13381) was first spoken at 6.5 steps, and why its clock was "wrong" (compare bbox centre vs contact point vs later frames). Report the first frame it was detected and confirmed as approaching.
3. **Beeps only on a real collision course:** |lateral| (static / unknown) or |predicted lateral at meeting| (moving) ≤ `beepLateralM` ≈ 0.9 m (lane half 0.6 + body margin), and ≤ 5 steps.
   - **Removed:** v26.1.12's "unknown motion within 5 steps / 3 m beeps" and "approaching within 5 steps / 3 m counts as collision course". All four beep ⊘ entries were at 1.2–3.0 m lateral.
   - **Kept:** review 5's 90.8 case (lat 2.1), reached by the speech at 5–10 steps instead; check it.
4. **Context beside the path:**
   - **Rule:** context obstacles need |lateral| ≤ `contextMaxLateralM`, lowered from 3.0. Pick a value from the review 4–6 fixtures that removes the 2.9 m ⊘ entries and keeps the ✓ ones, and report it.
   - **Fall-back:** if no value separates, use a combined rule (lateral and steps).
5. **Ego speed for motion state:** use a robust ego speed (median over ~3 s of the cadence-based speed, or the stride-held value) when classifying movers, so a momentary 2.07 spike doesn't flip "approaching" into "standing" (374.0). Report track 11195's state with the change.
