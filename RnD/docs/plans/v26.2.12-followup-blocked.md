## The request

## Why it does not fire (Opus diagnosis, 2026-11-09)

At 21.17 s a skip (dumpster) stands on the sidewalk ~6 m ahead, from the curb side to about the camera axis; the tree is left of
it, the free sidewalk continues right of it (between the skip and the scaffold fence / pole). The model labels the skip mostly
`barrier wall` (sometimes `sidewalk-obstacle`).

1. **Reach:** `blocked.aheadM` is 3.0 m; the skip is at 6.0–7.6 m from 20.0 s to 21.3 s.
2. **Evidence:** the fused map (`conf >= min_conf`) keeps only 1–16 cells of the skip and places them at right −1.6..−1.2 m at
   21.17 s, so they cover only 8–25 % of the lane (±0.6 m) in the 20.5–21.5 s window. The current frame's object bases see it in the
   lane (right edge −0.1..+0.5 m) but flicker frame to frame (lane share 0.00–0.58). The union of the solid bases of the last 0.7 s
   covers 83–92 % of the lane from 20.5 to 21.5 s (probe numbers, aheadM 8, all solid classes).
3. **Free direction:** `minAngleDeg` 15 forbids 12:15 (+7.5°), and the ray with the *longest* run is preferred, not the gentlest
   turn that passes the blockage.
4. **Phrase:** the template ("blocked ahead, sidewalk continues at X") does not name where the blockage is; the user's phrase does.

Risk if simply reaching further for all solid classes: buildings the user walks along/towards also fill the lane within 8 m
(17.8–18.5 s and 25.3–27.3 s, share 0.5–0.92); those are already beeped and were not asked for.

## The fix

Two blockage kinds in `_StaticMap.blocked_ahead` (src/collision.py) and `blocked` (common/config/collision.json):

**A. Walls** (`building`, `barrier fence`, `barrier guard-rail`): the v26.2.12 rule unchanged (fused map, `aheadM` 3.0, `widthFrac`
0.7). Their v26.2.12 firings must stay the same unless the phrase text changes (see D).

**B. Obstacles on the walkway** (new `blocked.obstacle`: classes `barrier`, `barrier wall`, `sidewalk-obstacle`, `divider`):
- Evidence = the fused map's obstacle cells (`conf >= min_conf`) **plus** the object-base points of those classes from the frames of
  the last `obstacle.recentS` 0.7 s, dead-reckoned into the current ego frame (fwd reduced by the distance walked since that frame,
  rotated by the heading change if the pose gives it; use `self.wm.last_pose` / the ego speed the pass already has). Keep the
  history inside `_StaticMap` (filled in `update()` from `b_row/b_col` and the grid classes).
- Reach `obstacle.aheadM` 6.5 m (≈ 9 steps), from `minAheadM` 0.4.
- Blocked when the evidence covers ≥ `obstacle.widthFrac` 0.5 of the lane's lateral bins (±laneHalf, 0.1 m bins).
- `confirmFrames`, `cooldownS`, `sameSpotM` as today (shared).

**C. Free direction** (both kinds): rays every `angleStepDeg` 7.5° within ±`angleDeg` 60°, skipping 0°. A ray is free when its
walkable run (same walkable rule as today) reaches past the blockage (≥ the blockage's farthest evidence fwd along the ray + 0.5 m,
and ≥ `minClearM` 2.0) without meeting solid map cells or obstacle evidence within `freeHalfWidthM` 0.25 m of the ray. Pick the
**smallest |angle|** free ray (ties: the side with more walkable cells). `minAngleDeg` is removed (or set to 7.5).

**D. Phrase** (the user's wording): `templates.free` = `"{label} blocked at {clock}, continues {free}"`,
`templates.none` = `"{label} blocked at {clock}"`. `{clock}` = clock text of the bearing of the blockage centroid (the evidence cells
within ±(laneHalf + 1.5 m) and within 1.5 m beyond the nearest evidence fwd); `{free}` = clock text of the chosen ray; `{label}` =
walkable label of the class under the user (the lane cells 0.5–2 m ahead, majority), default "sidewalk". The event's `blocked`
dict gains `kind` ("wall" | "obstacle") and `blockClock`.
