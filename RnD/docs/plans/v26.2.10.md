## Diagnosis (Opus, run 5fda5626e808 c2, Chest-0004)
- **3 people at 0 s, 1 on the map: the user is right.** Three people stand close together (two side by side, one against the wall);
  the segmentation gives one connected `person` region (249 cells of the 240 x 135 grid, cols 101–119, rows 39–60), so the pass makes one
  item (track 14, 6.0 m, −1.04 m) and the map one marker. A semantic model has no instances; the pass must split the region.
- **"Assumed terrain" = history that is no longer true, drawn where the camera sees something else.** At 10 s and 20 s the map shows
  road straight ahead while the frame sees sidewalk with the road to the left: the dead-reckoned heading drifts, the old road votes are
  rotated under the user, and the fusion *adds* the new sidewalk votes to them (6 s half-life), so the old class can still win a cell the
  camera sees right now. Nothing confronts the new observation with the stored one.
- **Forgetting what was seen:** every vote decays (half-life 6 s) whether the cell is visible or not, so a sidewalk hidden by a car for a
  few seconds fades.
- **Inference that is not a fact:** gap closing (`gapCloseM` 0.5: an unknown seam between a sidewalk and a wall becomes wall) and the
  wall thickness written away from the user (`faceThicknessM` 0.3: the back of a wall is never seen).
- **Re-run collision** (`POST /api/runs/{id}/collision`) is a blocking request (~70 s with the map): no job, no progress, no cancel.

## Design

### A. Map: facts only, confronted, persistent (`src/worldmap.py`, `common/config/worldmap.json`)
1. **Visible cells.** Per frame the Observer returns, beside `obs`, a `seen` mask of the observation grid: cells whose ground the frame
   actually sees (a surface pixel, or an object base written this frame). A cell whose image ray meets a mover (person, rider, animal,
   vehicle), an object above its base, or a building is **not seen** (occluded); nor is anything outside the field of view, nearer than the
   bottom image row, or beyond the observe range.
2. **No time decay** (`decay`: `"none"`, replaces `halfLifeSeconds`; keep the old path behind `decay: "time"` for A/B). A cell that is
   not seen keeps its votes unchanged: hidden by a person or a car, out of view, behind the user.
3. **Confrontation** in seen cells: before adding the new vote, the votes of every *other* class of the same layer (surface vs surface;
   object vs object; and a seen surface clears object votes as today) are multiplied by `conflictFactor` (0.4); the cell's total is capped
   at `voteCap` (6.0, two full-confidence weights) so that a long history cannot outvote the present for more than one or two frames. The
   existing negative evidence (`objectClearFactor`, rays, hidden surfaces) stays, now without decay.
4. **Range stays 20 m** (user, 2026-11-03: "don't store sidewalk or anything over 20m, since its the world map limit, the 40m reference
   was a way to explain not a request"). Nothing past `maxRangeM` 20 is observed or stored; the observation grid and the fusion window
   keep their sizes. Persistence (2, 3) is what keeps a hidden sidewalk within those 20 m.
5. **No inferred cells:** `gapCloseM` 0 (off); `faceThicknessM` 0.1 (one cell) for the cells written; the occlusion behind a building
   (`_hidden_cells`) keeps its own `hiddenThicknessM` 0.3 so the view rules do not change.
6. **Pose confronted with the map** (`poseAlign`, on): before fusing, search heading offsets ±`alignDeg` 2.0 (0.5° steps) and shifts
   ±`alignShiftM` 0.3 (0.1 m steps) of the dead-reckoned pose; score = the share of seen surface cells (to 20 m) whose stored class, where
   the map has one with confidence, equals the observed class. Take the best only when it beats the unshifted pose by `alignMinGain`
   (0.03) with at least `alignMinCells` (500) cells compared; the correction accumulates into the map's pose (it does not change the
   collision pass's own `_WorldMemory`). `pose.json` gains `alignDeg`, `alignM`, `agree` per frame.
7. **Metric (new, in `tools/` and the DECISIONS entry):** per frame *agreement* = the share of seen cells (to 20 m) where the snapshot's
   class equals the frame's own observation; report mean / p10 over Chest-0004 0–454 s for v26.2.9 (time decay) vs v26.2.10 (each of
   2, 3, 6 on and off), plus ms per frame. Expected: agreement up, the road-under-the-user at 10 s / 20 s gone.

### B. People that touch are separate items (`src/collision.py`)
- When the pass builds items from connected components, a `person` (and `rider`) component wider than `splitWidthFactor` 1.4 x the image
  width of a `personWidthM` 0.55 m person at its distance is split into columns: candidate cuts at local minima of the component's
  column height profile (the top edge per column; heads are peaks) and at depth jumps between columns of more than `splitDepthJumpM`
  0.6 m (depth map median per column over the component); pieces narrower than 0.25 m are merged into their neighbour. Each piece is an
  item with its own bbox / outline / distance / lateral / track. Config block `collision.json` `personSplit` (`enabled` true).
- Target: Chest-0004 at 0 s → 3 person items (± the 1-cell speck); at 5 s → 3. Single people on the fixtures must not be split: report
  person items per frame over the fixtures before / after and the frames whose count changed.
- Speech will change (more person items, grouped "person at 12 and 1 in 8"): re-pin the goldens and list the changed events in the
  DECISIONS entry (don't block on it).

### C. Lab
1. **Collision re-run as a job** (Note 1): `POST /api/runs/{id}/collision` returns at once with the new collision doc in status `running`
   (`progress` 0–1 from the pass's frame loop, then the map pass); the collision doc carries `status` / `progress` / `error`;
   `POST /api/runs/{id}/collisions/{cid}/cancel` stops it between frames (status `cancelled`, the partial directory removed, id not
   reused). One collision job at a time per Lab (409 otherwise; an analysis and a re-run never run together). The UI shows the same progress
   bar + Cancel as Analyse, inside that collision's row.
2. **Runs as groups** (Note 2): the runs list shows each run as a collapsible group (header = the run as it is shown today); its
   collisions c1, c2… are rows inside it, each with its label (editable), version, counts, status/progress, select, Delete. The Compare
   A/B panel picks from the same rows. The separate collision list elsewhere goes.
3. **Items selectable as a whole, hover info** (Note 3):
   - **Video:** hovering an item's outline / box shows a tooltip (class, role, track, distance in m and steps, clock, distance source,
     world track id + speed when there is one); a click selects the item: its whole outline is highlighted and it stays selected by track id
     while it lives; click on empty image or Esc clears.
   - **World map:** each item is drawn as its footprint (a short segment across its width at its distance, from its bbox edges through
     the lens, thick 2 px; movers keep the diamond at the centre) and hovering / clicking it behaves as on the video, with the same tooltip.
     A click on an object-layer cell (pole, wall, vegetation…) with no item selects the whole connected region of that class (8-connected
     cells of the snapshot shown), outlined, with hover info (class, cells, nearest distance, clock range).
   - **Linked:** selecting an item on one view selects it on the other (same frame, same track id).
   - The click readout under the map stays (cell lines), led by the selected item / region.
