## W1 (Sonnet, after W0): collision (`src/collision.py`, `src/sensors.py`, `src/worldmap.py`, `collision.json`)
1. **Defaults = c2 (note 2):** `source.mode: map`, `ego.headingSource: imu`, `horizon.source: fused`, `ego.cadenceSource: imu`.
   Explain in the report why c2's resolved `headingSource` said `imu+gps` with an `imu` override, and make the resolved label truthful.
2. **O18 step length from GPS:** new `ego.stepLength.source: auto` (`auto` | `prior`). With good GPS (`gps_good`, fix ≥ 3, DOP ≤ 2.5)
   over moving windows (≥ 10 s total, cadence known, not stationary): step length = median(GPS 2D speed / cadence), clamped
   [0.5, 1.1] m; it drives both the ego speed and the spoken steps. No good GPS → the prior (0.739 m at 1.78 m). A step length the user
   types in the form still wins. Resolved: `stepLengthM`, `stepLengthSource` (`gps` | `prior` | `user`), `gpsStepWindowsS`.
   Report the GPS step on every clip with good GPS (`tools/sensors_report.py` or a new flag). Chest-0004 has no fix, so it keeps the prior.
3. **Too close for voice (0.17):** a speech item whose spoken steps (predicted, or now if not predicted) are ≤
   `speech.minVoiceSteps` 2 and which is `collision` (beeped now or starting this frame) is dropped from the phrase; the beeps stay.
   Phrases with nothing left are not said.
4. **Speech only inside the collision zone for thin statics (1.17, 23.51):** `pole`, `sign`, `traffic sign`, `traffic-light*`,
   `sidewalk-obstacle`, `utility-cover` are spoken only when `collision` is true (inside the lane: |lateral| ≤ laneHalfWidthM + item
   radius), judged on the **current** position (not the predicted one). Multi-item phrases keep only the items that pass.
5. **Vehicles outside the lane (25.10):** a vehicle that is not measured as moving towards the user (world track `trusted` and speed
   < 0.3 m/s, or closing < 0.2 m/s, or motion unknown) is judged with the static lane, not the mover zone. Also find why 871's bbox was
   frozen while its distance changed (coast / ghost?) and fix it if it is a bug.
6. **Tree trunks and unstable thin items (18.34):** (a) a `pole` component whose top touches or sits under a `nature vegetation`
   component (canopy above, overlap in x ≥ 50 % of the pole's width, gap ≤ 2 grid rows) is a **tree**: label `tree`, family vegetation,
   spoken as "tree"; (b) a thin item whose distance changes by > 40 % (and > 1 m) between consecutive frames is not spoken until it has
   been stable for `speech.thinStableS` 0.5 s. Check whether the trunk's lateral error (camera yaw vs the walking direction) is the
   reason it fell in the lane; if the walking direction (IMU / map wall lines) can correct the lane, report it with numbers but **only
   implement it if it is small and safe**; otherwise log it as a known issue.
7. **Re-announce and closest marker (13.00):** (a) a static track already announced is announced again when its spoken place changed
   a lot (clock moved ≥ 2 half-hours **and** the item is `collision` now, or its distance grew by ≥ 3 steps: it now means another part
   of the wall); (b) co-located `collision` items (within 1.0 m in the map) are said once, using the **closest** item's distance and
   clock and its label (the user's rule). Target: a phrase like "building at 12 in 9" between 12.8 and 13.3 s.
8. **Walls being followed (21.00):** no new beep episode for a building / wall / fence face on the same side (left: clock ≤ 11:30,
   right: ≥ 12:30) when the same family on the same side had an episode in the last `beeps.followQuietS` 6 s and its lateral stayed
   within ±0.4 m of that episode's (the user is walking along it). Match by family + side + map position, not by trackId (265 → 800).
   A face that moves to the centre (11:45–12:15) or to ≤ 1 step still beeps.
9. **"Blocked ahead" (21.17):** from the map: when the lane ahead within `blocked.aheadM` 3.0 m is blocked by solid cells (building,
   wall, fence, barrier, obstacle) over ≥ 70 % of its width, and walkable ground (sidewalk, pedestrian area, crosswalk) continues on one
   side within ±60°, say "blocked ahead, sidewalk continues at 11" (the clock of the free direction, the walkable class name). Said once
   per blockage (5 s cooldown, world position). Also find why track 800 vanished at 21.17 s.
10. **Predictive speech (23.51, O19):** find the cause of the ~2 m over-advance (ego speed vs the measured 0.75 m/s from the pole's
    distance 23.5 → 25.1 s; dt; speech rate). Fix: the advance uses the measured ego speed and never exceeds `predictive.maxAdvanceM`
    1.5 m; the spoken clock stays the current clock unless the predicted one differs by ≤ 1 half-hour (no swing to 1:15 from 12:15).
    The new default speech rate 1.25 (note 4) shortens dt.
11. **Horizon on the image (note 3):** hazards items carry `bboxFull` and `outlineFull`: the item's whole connected component including
    rows above the horizon (decisions unchanged, so events stay identical for this change). W2 uses them.
12. **Speech rate default 1.25 (note 4):** `tones.speechRate` 1.25 in `collision.json` (it feeds predictive dt).
