## Why

DA2 metric depth squashes range beyond about 12 m (DECISIONS v26.2.2, known issue 5). Depth divided by ground distance is:

| Ground distance | 0–8 m | 8–12 m | 12–16 m | 16–20 m | 20–30 m | 30–60 m |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ratio | 1.45 | 1.27 | 1.03 | 0.84 | 0.64 | 0.39 |

Because of this, distant buildings and fences land at 6–12 m on the map and make false wall faces (candidates 46, 70, 118 and 168, and the edge or unsure events).

The horizon ground ray (`H·f / ((y − horizon)·gh)`) does not have this squashing. Its error grows near the horizon, though: at about 30 m one grid row is worth about a third of the distance, against about 10% at 10 m. The map already has `objectDistance: "ground-contact"`, which has never been measured.

## Build

### 1. `distance.objectDistance`: "depth" | "ground-contact" | "ground+depth"

This is one setting, used by both the collision items (`detect_items` measurements and the snap) and the world map object bases (`worldmap.object_bases`). The world map must keep using exactly what the collision pass used. "ground+depth" works as follows:

- **Range correction of depth.**
  - **Fit:** per frame, a correction curve r(d) = depth / ground-ray distance, binned by depth. Samples are walking-region surface pixels whose ground distance is within the reliable band, where `(y − horizon)·gh ≥ minRowsBelowHorizon`, about 4 rows; put it in config.
  - **Smoothing:** smooth the curve over time (EMA about 1 s) and hold it about 2 s when there are too few samples.
  - **Apply:** apply it to every depth reading of objects, movers included: corrected = depth / r(depth).
  - **Beyond the fit:** past the farthest bin, extrapolate the last ratio or use the configured prior table above; measure which works better.
- **Fusion per object base.**
  - **Weighting:** when the base stands on a surface (contact) and is inside the reliable band, combine the ground ray and the corrected depth with inverse-variance weights. The ground-ray variance comes from the horizon jitter: σ_y is measured from the stored horizon track, and σ_D/D = σ_y / (y − horizon). The depth variance is a configured relative error.
  - **No contact:** with no contact (occluded base, base not on a surface) or outside the band, use the corrected depth only.
- **Sanity gate.** A base that stands on a surface cannot be nearer than about 0.7 × its ground-ray distance; put that factor in config. If depth claims that, the ground ray wins. Base-not-on-surface cases are excluded, because occlusion makes the ground ray an upper bound there.
- **Hazards JSON.** Per item, record `distanceSource` ("depth" | "ground" | "fused"), `rawDepthM` and `groundM`. Per frame, record the correction curve, as a short array, for the Lab.

### 2. Horizon on/off: `distance.useHorizon` true | false

- **true:** today's behaviour, plus option 1.
- **false:**
  - **Distances:** no ground ray in any object or mover distance. The correction curve and fusion are off, so distance = depthScale × DA2 only.
  - **Map:** the world map object bases and the surface projection also use depth instead of the horizon. Surfaces are projected through the depth map with the lens ray model.
  - **Depth scale:** if a part cannot work without the horizon, keep it on the horizon and list it in DECISIONS. The depth-scale calibration (`initial` / gait anchor) is the likely case, since it is the only metric reference. State clearly what "off" removes and what it keeps.
- **Per run:** this is a per-run option, exposed in the Lab settings next to the body settings (the same place as camera location and side), together with `objectDistance`. The collision re-run and the map rebuild pick it up. Add both to the CONTRACTS run or collision settings.

### 3. Defaults

The defaults are `objectDistance: "ground+depth"` and `useHorizon: true`. If the measurement shows ground+depth is clearly worse than depth, keep it as the default anyway only if the user's ✓ results are no worse; otherwise default to "depth" and say so. Either way, ship it. Misses go under Known / possible issues (user rule, 2026-10-25: don't block).
