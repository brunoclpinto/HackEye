## Decisions
1. **Two notification kinds**, emitted on tone / beeps events as `kind`:
   - `collision`: today's parking beeps (880 Hz, interval by steps), unchanged.
   - `proximity` (new): a person or animal measured **approaching** (motion state, not unknown / standing / receding), within `proximitySteps` 5 steps and |lateral| ≤ `proximityLateralM` 2.6 m, **not** on a collision course. Riders and vehicles never get it: they are spoken early, and collision beeps cover them.
2. **The proximity sound** is clearly different and softer:
   - **Tone:** lower pitch (≈ 440 Hz), a soft sine with a slower attack, about 50% of the collision gain.
   - **Rhythm:** a fixed slow rhythm (one soft double-chirp per ~1.2 s) rather than the accelerating parking rate. The rate doesn't encode distance; speech already did that at 5–10 steps.
   - **Switching:** when an item turns into a collision course, it switches to collision beeps; the two sounds never play for one track at once.
   - **Config:** `tones.proximity` (frequencyHz, gain, intervalSeconds, chirp spec).
3. **Lab:**
   - **Playback:** `audio.js` plays the event's frequency, gain and chirp from the event, so desktop and mobile get both sounds.
   - **Notification review:** the panel shows the kind (e.g. "soft · person at 1:30 · 4→2 steps"), and reviewers can verdict them like beeps. The ⊘ / added flow is unchanged.
   - **Tones checkbox:** it covers both kinds.
4. **Expected on review 6:** the 6 lost ✓ person beeps come back as proximity sounds where the person was measured approaching. The ⊘ person beeps at 203.8 and 376.0 s may also come back as proximity sounds; the user accepted that trade-off by choosing (c). The ⊘ cyclist beep at 191.5 s stays silent (riders excluded), and so does 180.7 s (3.0–3.6 m lateral, outside the zone). Report each.
