## Decisions
1. **The player owns a queue** (`audio.js`): only one utterance at a time is handed to `speechSynthesis`.
   - **Priority:** final > danger (incl. approach) > feature / thin > surface > context.
   - **Preemption:** a phrase of higher priority than the one speaking **cancels it and speaks at once**. Equal or lower priority waits in the player queue.
   - **No late speech:** a waiting phrase is **dropped** if it can't start within `lateSeconds` (1.0 s of media time) of its event time. Log `speech-dropped-late` and `speech-preempted` to the client log and the audio log.
   - **Hard-won behaviour:** keep the iOS / Mac / watchdog / stuck-notice behaviour. The watchdog must not count a deliberate preempt-cancel as a failure, and the stuck notice must not fire from preemption.
   - **Review edits:** "Hear review edits" (added phrases) go through the same queue.
2. **No motion wait while the ego speed isn't settled.** For people, `_motion_pending` doesn't apply until the robust ego speed has a full window (≥ 3 s of cadence samples after the warm-up). Until then the plain phrase is said at once.
3. **Motion words need a settled ego speed.** standing / approaching / moving away are used only when the robust ego speed has its full window. Otherwise the state is unknown (plain phrase). That makes 3.2 s go away: the person is said once, at ~2.0 s, as "person at 12 in 9".
4. **Expected on review 7:** one person phrase at ~2.0 s (plain or "standing", not "approaching"); nothing for that person at 3.2 s; the 4 ✓ entries kept. Check the other v26.1.14 runs (Chest-0002, Chest-0001) for side effects in their first seconds.
