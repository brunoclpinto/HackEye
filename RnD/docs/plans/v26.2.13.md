## Changes (W1, src/collision.py + common/config/collision.json)

0. **Fixture first, before anything else:** `tests/fixtures/chest0004_review10.{json,npz}` from run `5fda5626e808` c1 (the Lab dir
   `debug/lab/runs/5fda5626e808`), the same layout and README text style as `chest0004_review9` (one window w0 = 0–72 s, motion to
   150 s, sensors to 156 s, depth float16 exactly as stored), `overrides` {} (c2 is the default now), `speechRate` 1.25, the
   annotations + notes from `tests/fixtures/chest0004_review10_export.json` (Opus saved the export there; drop that raw file once the
   fixture holds them), the c1 system events as `systemEventsV26_2_12`. Check: `tests/replay.py` on it reproduces the c1 events in
   0–72 s (same t/text); if not exact, say why.
1. **Walls being followed stay quiet (speech and beeps), any clock:** a wall-family item (building, wall, fence: the families
   `beeps.follow` already uses) is *followed* when over the last `follow.windowS` 1.5 s its distance closed by less than
   `follow.minCloseRatio` 0.4 × the distance walked (ego speed × time; needs ≥ `follow.minWalkM` 0.5 m walked). A followed wall
   starts no beep episode and gets no speech phrase. It is still beeped at ≤ 1 step (`final`), and a wall that starts closing again
   (ratio ≥ 0.4 over the window) is no longer followed. Keep the v26.2.12 `beeps.follow` rule too (it still covers new faces of the
   same wall).
2. **Known wall position:** a wall speech phrase is not said again when its nearest world point (the pose the pass already keeps) is
   within `speech.knownWallM` 2.0 m of the world point of a wall phrase or beep episode of the same family in the last
   `speech.knownWallS` 30 s (44.66 vs 34.83).
3. **"moved away":** an item that was a danger with `collision` True (or beeped) in the last `away.recentS` 2.0 s and now leaves the
   collision lane by its own motion (|lateral| grows past laneHalf + `away.marginM` 0.2 m with lateral speed ≥ `away.minLatMps`
   0.2 m/s away from the axis, or its distance grows while the user walks) is said once: "person at 12:30 moved away"; when it was
   not moving (`moving` False for ≥ `away.staticS` 1.0 s) just before, "person at 12:15 started walking away" (non-people: "started
   moving away"). Role `danger`, priority like a danger phrase; once per track per `away.cooldownS` 10 s. Targets: 30.16 ± 0.7 s
   (text "person at 12:15 started walking away"; clock may be 12:15/12:30), 40.34 ± 0.7 s ("person at 12:30 moved away";
   12:30/12:45).
4. **Beeps for people passing close:** a moving person (or rider / vehicle) that is approaching (closing ≥ `passing.minClosingMps`
   0.8 m/s) and within `passing.lateralM` 1.5 m laterally and ≤ `passing.maxSteps` 4 steps gets the soft beeps (the existing soft kind,
   pan to its side) even outside the lane, until it is passed. Target: soft beeps for track 1575 within 60.0–61.3 s; no new beeps
   for static items outside the lane (O25 stays as it is).
5. Tests: a review-10 fixture test (targets 1–4: the 12 removes gone, the corrects kept, the two added phrases within ±0.7 s, beeps
   for 1575), unit tests per rule, older fixtures through `tools/collision_score.py` before/after (ticks must not drop; list every
   changed event). The review-9 "blocked" test must still pass.
