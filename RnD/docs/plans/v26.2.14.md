0. **Fixture first:** `tests/fixtures/chest0004_review11.{json,npz}` from run `5fda5626e808` c1, layout like `chest0004_review10`
   (README text in `source`), with the annotations + notes from `tests/fixtures/chest0004_review11_export.json` (Opus saved it; drop
   the raw file once the fixture holds them) and the c1 events as `systemEventsV26_2_13`. Windows: as few as keep the npz small (one
   0–240 s window, or several windows each starting ≥ 10 s before its first target if their replays match c1 after the warm-up); motion and
   sensors to the end the replay needs (see review 9's note: the gyro bias / GPS need ~150 s). Record in the json that every
   unreviewed c1 event before 233.5 s counts as ✓ (`implicitCorrectBefore`: 233.5). Check that the replay reproduces c1 (with the new rules off).
1. **Own motion of a mover** (person, rider, vehicle, animal): a robust estimate per item from its own relative positions over the last
   `motion.windowS` 1.2 s (fwd, right per frame; own velocity = d(fwd)/dt + ego speed, d(right)/dt; a line fit with outlier rejection,
   across track fragments when the world track links them), with its uncertainty. "Moving" when the own speed is ≥ `motion.minSpeedMps`
   (person 0.4, vehicle 0.8) and significant. Use it, not the closing rate alone, for:
   - **Approaching** (70.84): the "approaching" word needs own motion toward the user (≥ the min speed). A parked vehicle is static.
   - **Near-collision course** (59.16, 69.34): an approaching mover whose predicted lateral at passing (right + v_right × t_pass,
     t_pass = fwd / closing speed) is within `nearPassM` 1.1 m and t_pass ≤ `nearPassS` 4 s is a collision danger. It gets collision
     beeps by steps (not the soft sound), and is said again as it closes: "person approaching at 11:30 in 3" when its steps drop by
     ≥ 2 since its last phrase (≥ 1.5 s gap). It is said early, from the first frames where the course holds (target: by 58.5 s for the 59.16
     person, beeps until it passes ~61 s; 69.34 the same).
   - **From a side** (233.5): when the own lateral speed is ≥ `motion.sideMps` (0.5 m/s), the phrase names where it comes from:
     "vehicle approaching from right at 12:30 in 5" (moving leftwards = from right). Every approaching phrase carries "in <steps>".
   - **Cutting in front** (141.0): a person within `cut.maxSteps` 4 steps ahead whose own lateral motion takes it into the lane
     within `cut.withinS` 1.5 s, after it was outside the lane or moving another way: "person cutting in front", role final, once
     per track per 10 s.
   - **Moving away ahead** (149.37): a person in the lane ahead, walking the same way (own forward speed ≥ 0.4 m/s away from the user, its gap
     steady or growing) for ≥ `ahead.stableS` 1.0 s, said once after a cut-in or when it first settles in front within 5 steps:
     "person moving away at 12 in 4" (the existing wording).
   If vehicle motion can't be measured (233.5's tracks), say so with numbers; don't invent motion.
