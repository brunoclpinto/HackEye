# engine/worldmap -- the fused bird's-eye world map

The world map fuses the class grid, the depth map and the frame's horizon and ego block into an ego-centric map. This
README covers the whole model (p3-01 to p3-08): the config loader, the pose integration, the hazards helpers, the observer, the fusion,
the `WorldMap` driver and the face / edge test. Imports only numpy, the stdlib and `engine.core`.

**Fields written** (`engine.core.types.FIELD_WRITERS`): owns `Item.map_source`, `Item.map_cells`, `Item.map_world`, `Item.image_dl`, `Item.solid_corrected`, `Item.raw_distance_m`, `Item.inside_solid`, `Item.ghost`, `Item.map_judged`, `Item.face_edge`, `Item.srcsel`, `Item.track_motion`, `Item.pred_v`, `Track.ghost_src`, `Track.ghost_t0`; also writes `Item.distance_m`, `Item.lateral_m`, `Item.bearing`, `Item.clock`, `Item.pan`, `Item.clipped`, `Item.closing`, `Item.role`, `Item.urgency`, `Item.summary_ok`, `Item.speak`, `Item.collision`, `Item.proximity`, `Item.shape`, `Item.clock_q`, `Item.clock_text`, `Item.full_ref`.

### 7.4 Items — walls on the map, inside solid, ghost

- **Walls judged on the map** (v26.2.2, `staticSource: "map"`; replaces the v26.2.1 building section and the v26.1.16 lane part, which are no longer emitted): a static item of `mapJudgedClasses` is judged on the fused world map (§8.1) alone. Its footprint = the map cells of its own ground-base points grown by `mapFootprintRadiusM`; its cells = fused object cells of its family there with confidence ≥ `mapMinConf` inside the full corridor (`|right| ≤ laneHalfWidthM`, ahead > 0). The **face / edge test** (`src/engine/worldmap/faceedge.py`) classifies each cell: *edge* = within `edgeBandM` (+ blur per metre) of the wall line of its side (RANSAC line of the family's cells 0-8 m ahead, |angle| ≤ 25°; a cell on a side without a line is judged against the other side's line); *face* = not an edge and protruding past its line toward the axis by ≥ `faceProtrusionM`, or in a local cluster whose principal axis runs across the path (≥ 45°), or with no wall line at all. With ≥ `minFaceCells` face cells (and the user not standing still) the item is a lane danger and takes the nearest face cell as its measurement (distance = ahead, lateral = right, bearing = atan2(right, ahead), clock, steps); the image values are kept as `imageDistanceM` / `imageLateralM`. Edge cells, a wall beside the path, or too few face cells: no lane danger (the item stays context as before). With no cell of the item in the corridor the image rule decides (`mapImageFallback`). Poles, signs, sidewalk obstacles and parked vehicles keep the v26.2.0 image rule. Hazards JSON: `faceEdge` per static item with corridor cells (verdict, counts, `nearestFaceM`, `lines`, `faceCells` / `edgeCells` (≤ 60 each, forward / right m), `stationary`, image lane flags) and `faceEdgeLines` per frame.
- **Inside solid** (v26.2.2, `insideSolid.mode`): a non-wall item (static of another class, mover, coasting mover) whose map position lies more than `solidDepthM` inside the solid wall-like cells (fused, confidence ≥ `mapMinConf`) or behind a face of ≥ `minRayCells` consecutive solid cells on its own ray is a depth error: with mode `"snap"` its distance / lateral / bearing / clock are moved along its own bearing to the first solid cell of the ray (the near border), `distanceM`, `stepsTo` and every later rule use the corrected point; JSON `insideSolid: true`, `solidCorrected: true`, `rawDistanceM`. Mode `"suppress"` gives it no role, speech, beeps or proximity sound instead (A/B). A coasting mover that drifts into solid stops coasting.
- **Ghost** (v26.2.1): a confirmed static danger (`role_run ≥ confirmFrames`) that is clipped or within `mapGhostNearM` is remembered with its world position. When the image loses its track, a ghost item (a copy, `ghost: true`, `clipped`, no outline) is emitted each frame as long as fused object cells of its family (confidence ≥ `mapGhostMinConf`) are in the corridor near the dead-reckoned position, for ≤ `mapGhostSeconds`, until passed (`ahead ≤ 0`); it keeps the track, so the danger, beeps and the final call continue.

### 7.11c Collision source and predictive speech — the source

- **Source** (`source.mode` `image` default | `map`; Lab override `source.mode`): `image` = every item's position is the image measurement (byte-identical to v26.2.10 with predictive off). `map` (needs `roles.staticSource` "map"; else it falls back to `image`) decides per REAL item where its distance / lateral / clock come from (`item.srcsel`, `hazards[].source` = `map` | `track` | `image`, only written in map mode; `settings.sourceMode` "map" + `settings.sourceCounts`):
  - **map** (statics not judged by the face / edge test): `_StaticMap.region`: the 8-connected region of fused object cells of the item's family (confidence >= `roles.mapMinConf`) that the item's ground footprint touches, searched in a patch grown by `source.regionPadM` 2; needs >= `regionMinCells` 2 cells; the nearest cell (forward + 1e-3 |right|) gives the position, the cells inside the walking corridor give `map_cells` (so a region in the lane is a lane item), width across the line of sight. Judged wall-like classes keep their v26.2.2 map judgement (`source` map when the map placed them, else image). The image item stays the carrier of the identity, bbox / outline, confirmation and the speech bookkeeping; the memory (`_WorldMemory`) links it by the new position.
  - **track** (movers, group `moving`): position = the TRUSTED world track (`WorldTracks.lookup`, predicted to this frame on its velocity) in the corrected pose (`_StaticMap.pose`); its world velocity gives `closing` (gap shrink rate with our own speed), `latVelMps`, `crossMps` and the own speed towards us used by the motion state (no extra smoothing). Uses the previous frame's track state (causal). Coasting movers follow their trusted world track too (`source.coastFromTracks`).
  - **image safety net**: an item within `imageNetSteps` 3 steps, a track younger than `imageNetFrames` 2 frames, a mover whose world track is not trusted or sits more than max(`maxTrackJumpM` 3, `maxTrackJumpFrac` 0.35 x distance) from the image position stays `image`. Roles, side limits and step thresholds are unchanged.
  - World tracks are fed the image measurement (`imageDistanceM` / `imageLateralM`), never what a track placed itself.

### 7.11d v26.2.12 collision rules — blocked ahead

- **Obstacle width for "blocked", v26.2.14** (`blocked.obstacle.minWidthM` 0.8, `minWidthFrames` 2): an obstacle "blocked" phrase is said only when one obstacle was seen >= `minWidthM` wide (lateral extent of its bases in one frame) in >= `minWidthFrames` recent frames.
- **Blocked ahead** (`blocked`: `enabled`, `aheadM` 3, `minAheadM` 0.4, `widthFrac` 0.7, `wallClasses`, `obstacle` {`classes`, `aheadM` 6.5, `widthFrac` 0.5, `recentS` 0.7}, `confirmFrames` 2, `cooldownS` 5, `sameSpotM` 3, `clearS` 2, `angleDeg` 60, `angleStepDeg` 7.5, `minClearM` 2, `freeHalfWidthM` 0.1, `walkMinConf`, `solidClasses`, `walkableClasses`, `walkableLabels`, `templates`): `_StaticMap.blocked_ahead`. The lane (|lateral| <= lane half width, 0.1 m lateral bins) is blocked by (a) a **wall** (`wallClasses` map cells, confidence >= `mapMinConf`) within `aheadM` over >= `widthFrac` of its bins, or else (b) an **obstacle on the walkway** (`obstacle.classes`: their map cells plus the object-base points of the last `recentS` s, dead-reckoned to the current pose) within `obstacle.aheadM` over >= `obstacle.widthFrac`. The free direction is the ray (every `angleStepDeg`, within +-`angleDeg`, not 0) with the smallest turn whose walkable run reaches 0.5 m past the blockage (and >= `minClearM`) with no solid cell or obstacle point within `freeHalfWidthM`. The blockage clock is the bearing of its evidence centroid (within lane half width + 1.5 m, up to 1.5 m past its nearest point); the label is the walkable class under the user (lane 0.5-2 m ahead), default "sidewalk". Said as a `feature` speech event (`blocked: {kind, share, clearM, bearingDeg, clock, blockClock, endClass, cfwd, cright}`) "sidewalk blocked at 11:45, continues 12:15" (or "sidewalk blocked at 11:45" without a free direction), after `confirmFrames` frames of the same kind, only when no other phrase is due, at most every `cooldownS`, not at the same spot (`sameSpotM`), and once per blockage: a new phrase needs the blockage gone for `clearS` s.

### 8.1 World map  (`src/engine/worldmap/`, `common/config/worldmap.json`, `lab/stages/worldmap.py` `worldmap_pass`; v26.2.0 spike, read by the collision pass since v26.2.1)


A fused, ego-centric bird's-eye map built from the stored files of a run (`grids.npz`, `depth.npz`, and the horizon / ego block / calibration of `hazards.json`); no inference. Since v26.2.1 the collision pass runs the same incremental update online (`worldmap.WorldMap.step` with the frame's rounded ego values, causal), so the stored `worldmap.npz` equals the map the collision rules saw (test `test_online_map_equals_the_offline_build`); cost about 14 ms/frame (v26.2.7; was 11). File `runs/<id>/collisions/<cid>/worldmap.npz` (compressed; v26.2.8: one per collision run, built from that run's `hazards.json` with its stored overrides, §5.1): `classes` (N,200,200) uint8 (255 = unknown; class ids of the run's `classNames`), `conf` (N,200,200) uint8, `surface` / `object` (N,200,200) uint8 (v26.2.4: the winning surface class and the winning object class of each cell on their own, 255 = none; `classes` stays the one-class-per-cell argmax the collision rules read), `t` (N,), `pose` (N,3: x east m, y north m, heading deg; 0 = initial direction, + = right turn), `cellM` (0.10), `origin` ([100, 200] = the user's column, row: the bottom edge), `ms` (N, per-frame cost of observation + fusion + snapshot), and since v26.2.10 `alignDeg`, `alignM`, `agree`, `agreePrior` (N,; see Facts only / Agreement below).

- **Snapshot geometry (v26.2.4, forward only):** 200 × 200 cells of 0.10 m (20 × 20 m work area), heading-up: forward is up, the user stands on the bottom edge, column 100 / row 200 (20 m ahead, ±10 m to the sides, nothing behind; was row 180 with 2 m behind). Cell (row, col) is `(200 - row) · 0.1` m ahead and `(col - 100) · 0.1` m right of the body centre line (the same lateral convention as `hazards[].lateralM`).
- **Scope rule (user, 2026-10-25; 2026-11-03: "don't store sidewalk or anything over 20m, since its the world map limit"):** the map observes and stores nothing past `maxRangeM` (20); the observation grid and the fusion window keep their sizes (the 64 m window is only the room to recentre in). Hazards / speech keep their rules; the snapshot and the click readout cover only the work area (v26.2.6: the readout no longer lists items beyond 20 m: it says only what the selected cell holds). Errors past 20 m are a known limitation, not bugs.
- **Rules behind the user:** no collision rule reads snapshot rows behind the user (`corr_full` needs `ahead > 0`, the ghost is "passed" at `ahead <= 0` from the dead-reckoned world position, solid rays start ahead); the internal fusion grid is unchanged.
- **Layers and cell endpoint (v26.2.4):** `GET /api/runs/{id}/worldmap` (v26.2.7) adds `faceClassIds` and `surfaceClassIds` (class ids of the run's `classNames` that are wall faces / surfaces on the map) for the Lab's wall line. `GET /api/runs/{id}/worldmap/cell?i=&col=&row=` returns `{i, t, col, row, aheadM, rightM, layers, combined, conf, surface, object}` (class ids or null; `layers:false` for maps built before v26.2.4: `surface`/`object` null, only `combined`); 422 outside the 200 × 200 map, 404 unknown frame.
- **PNG encoding** (`worldmap/{i}.png`): default 8-bit **RGBA**, R = G = B = class id (the browser reads the red channel exactly as for `classes.png`), alpha 255 where the cell is known and 0 where it is unknown. `alpha=conf`: alpha = confidence 0..255 (canvases premultiply: the class id is only exact where alpha is 255; use it for display only). `alpha=gray`: single channel, class ids, 255 = unknown (no confidence). `Cache-Control: max-age=86400`; cached server-side for 1 run, keyed by mtime.
- **v26.2.3:** object bases are placed by `settings.objectDistance` (`groundfuse.resolve` per base pixel, with the frame's `depthCorrection`, §7.3b) and `useHorizon: false` projects the surfaces through the depth map instead of the horizon (no depth: the horizon path stays, no object bases); `POST /api/runs/{id}/collision` rebuilds a `worldmap.npz` that already exists (v26.2.6: always rebuilds it).
- **Projection:** surface classes (`worldmap.json` `classes.surface`) by inverse ground-plane sampling (each map cell -> the image pixel that sees it; camera height x scale, `lateralOffsetM`, per-frame `horizonY`; scale 1.0 when `distanceSource` is depth, else the frame's gait `scale`) **through the lens (v26.2.7, `lateralModel` "geometry", the default: fisheye runs with depth distances: `groundfuse.ground_pixel`, the exact inverse of `ground_point`, see below)**; a rectilinear run, a ground-distance run and the A/B models `collision` / `lens` keep the collision pass's pinhole formulas (`focal_px`, `ground_distance_m` / `lateral_m` inverted; exact on a pinhole image, and for ground distances the gait scale already absorbs the lens factor). Objects (`classes.object`): only their base pixels (lowest pixel of each vertical run; a base standing on `nature sky` is dropped), at the **median depth of the bottom `faceDepthRows` (6) rows of the run** (same class, same column) x the frame's `depthScale` (the metres `distanceM` uses) when the run has depth, else at the ground distance of the row (only bases with surface below). The ground+depth / ground-contact fusion (§7.3b) applies only to bases that stand on a surface; other bases keep the depth median. Movers (`classes.mover`) are never mapped.
- **Wall faces (v26.2.5, `faceClasses` = building, barrier wall / fence / guard-rail, divider, barrier, structure bridge / tunnel):** the base points of a face class are smoothed per class over image columns: the distance is the running median over ±`faceSmoothCols` (3) columns, and a base further than `faceOutlierFrac` (0.25) from it is dropped (`Observer.object_bases` returns the kept, smoothed points; the collision rules read the same points). On the map, kept bases of adjacent columns (≤ `faceJoinCols` apart, distances within `faceJoinFrac` = 0.2) are joined by a straight segment, and every face point or segment is `faceThicknessM` thick (0.3 m until v26.2.9, 0.1 m since v26.2.10), extended away from the user along the ray, so a facade is one continuous line. Non-face objects (poles, signs, vegetation...) keep the single-base splat.
- **Lens ground geometry (v26.2.7, `src/engine/groundfuse/`; fisheye runs with depth distances):** the collision pass's pinhole formulas put the ground 1.2 - 1.8 times too near on the 118 deg fisheye (a sidewalk drawn short of the building: the "unmapped gap") and the lateral ~45 % too wide a quarter of the width off centre. One lens geometry now serves the map's surfaces, the object bases and the items: `ray_dir` (the pixel's normalised ray, equidistant fisheye or pinhole), `ground_point(geo, row, col, horizon[, cam_h])` -> (forward m, right m) of the floor point seen there (z = `ground_z`, forward = z (cos phi - yn sin phi), right = xn z, phi = the pitch the horizon row implies; nan above the horizon), `ground_pixel` (its exact inverse, < 0.01 px / 1 mm round trip), `lateral_at(geo, row, col, fwd, horizon)` (the pixel ray's right offset at a forward distance). The camera height is `geo.cam_h` or the `cam_h` argument (the gait-scaled height where the caller uses it). Map: a surface cell (fwd, right - `lateralOffsetM`) takes the class of `ground_pixel`'s pixel; an object base's lateral is `lateral_at` at its distance (+ `lateralOffsetM`); a base with no depth takes the `ground_point` forward distance of its base row; the `objectClearBottomM` band starts at the forward distance of the last image row on the centre column. Items: see `distance.itemLens` in §7.3b (default: the lateral of an item with a depth distance is `lateral_at`; `"all"` also routes `detect_items` / `_lane_part_item` ground distances through `collision._ground_dist` / `_lateral_of`); `bearing_deg` / the clock were already lens-correct. `collision.ground_distance_m` / `lateral_m` stay for the pinhole cases (rectilinear runs, ground-distance runs and the ground-distance fallback, which must not change).
- **Building edge at the sidewalk limit (v26.2.7, `faceGroundContact: true`, `faceScaleOccluded: true`):** a face-class base (above) that stands on a surface takes the lens ground-contact distance of its base row (>= `groundDepth.minRowsBelowHorizon` rows below the horizon, ground <= `maxRangeM`), the geometry the surface projection uses, so the sidewalk and the building meet in one cell (with `depth` and with the ground modes alike). The facade's other bases (standing on a car, pole, sign...) follow: per frame and face class ratio = median(ground / depth) over its contact bases (at least `faceRatioMinBases` (10), clamped to `faceRatioClamp` [0.6, 1.5]); those bases take depth x ratio, else keep the depth. Done before the column smoothing.
- **Buildings block the view (v26.2.7, `occluderClasses` ["building"], `surfaceHiddenFactor` 0.3, `hiddenBinDeg` 0.5):** per frame, the cells behind an occluder's face points (from range + `faceThicknessM` outwards along the bearing, per `hiddenBinDeg` bearing bin the nearest occluder range, gaps of up to two empty bins bridged) are hidden (`Observer.ray_hidden`) minus the cells this frame itself sees as surface (visible ground wins). A world cell that maps to a hidden ego cell multiplies its stored SURFACE votes by `surfaceHiddenFactor` (object votes untouched; same place as the stale-object clearing), so a street cannot stay mapped through a facade.
- **Gap closing (v26.2.7, `gapCloseM`; off (0) since v26.2.10, it was inference):** in `Fusion.snapshot` (so the collision pass and the stored map agree), an unknown cell whose line toward the user meets a surface cell, and whose line away from the user meets a face-class cell, both within `gapCloseM`, takes the nearest face cell's class and confidence, in the combined map and in the object layer (the surface layer is left alone). A ground and a wall do not have a gap between them; seams wider than `gapCloseM` stay unknown.
- **Wall calibration of the depth (v26.2.7, `groundDepth.wallSampleWeight` 5; `worldmap.face_contact_samples`):** the collision pass passes (scaled depth, lens ground z) of each frame's face-class bases that stand on a surface (same base / contact rules and bottom-`faceDepthRows` median depth as the object bases, never range-corrected; >= `minRowsBelowHorizon` rows below the horizon, ground z <= `fuseMaxM`) to `Corrector.update(extra=...)`. They join the range bins only (never `ref`, which stays the walking region's near field), each base counting `wallSampleWeight` towards `minBinSamples`, with the weighted median in a bin, so the curve reaches the walls' range (12 - 22 m) where the walking region stops (~12.5 m). The `depthCorrection` report adds `"wallSamples": n` (omitted when 0). Only the ground modes read the curve.
- **Depth scale fallback (v26.2.5):** a frame without `depthScale` (the plane anchor needs `initialAnchorMinFrames` valid frames, so the collision pass seeds frames 0-1 with the scale of their own plane fit) uses the nearest known scale of the run: offline `build` the first known one before any, then the last known one; online the last known one, none until the first (`WorldMap.resolve_frame`).
- **Pose:** `collision._WorldMemory.advance` fed with `ego.speedMps` (0 when `stationary`) and `ego.headingDeg`.
- **Fusion:** world window 64 m (0.10 m cells, recentred when the user is within 22 m of an edge); class votes weighted by range (`1 / (1 + (d / 8 m)^2)`), cell class = argmax when the weight is above `weightFloor` (0.25) (v26.2.10: no time decay by default, see **Facts only** below; `decay: "time"` = the v26.2.9 exponential decay, half-life `halfLifeSeconds` 6 s, implemented lazily); `conf` = 255 · min(1, weight / 3) · vote share. **Objects are not hidden by the surface (v26.2.5):** when the argmax is a surface class but the object layers hold at least `objectWinShare` (0.35) of the cell's weight (and the decayed object weight is above the floor), the cell is the winning object class and `conf` = 255 · min(1, object weight / 3) · the class's share among the objects. The separate `surface` / `object` layers are unchanged. **Negative evidence (v26.2.5, `Fusion._clear_stale_objects`):** every frame, the object-class votes of a world cell whose current observation is a surface class (visible ground) are multiplied by `objectClearFactor` (0.3; the stored pre-multiplied votes scale linearly, so the lazy decay stays correct); surface votes are untouched. Not cleared: cells within `objectClearMarginM` (0.3 m) of any cell the frame writes as an object (face lines and thickness included), and cells nearer than the ground distance of the image's last row + `objectClearBottomM` (0.5 m) (an object whose base left the bottom of the image stands just nearer than the nearest visible ground, so the pole ghost survives). With `objectClearRays` (1): the cells on the ray from the user to each current face point, from `minForwardM` to the face distance minus the margin, are free space too even where no ground is visible (hidden behind a mover); the clear mask is dilated by one cell so that adjacent rays leave no gaps.
- **Facts only (v26.2.10, `src/engine/worldmap/`; plan docs/plans/v26.2.10.md A):** the map shows what the camera has seen and nothing it has guessed. (1) **Seen cells:** `Observer.observe` leaves `Observer.seen` (bool, 200 x 200) = the cells whose ground (a surface pixel, by the inverse projection) or whose object base this frame actually sees; a cell whose image ray meets a mover, an object above its base or a building, a cell outside the field of view, nearer than the last image row, or beyond `maxRangeM`, is not seen. (2) **No time decay** (`decay: "none"`, the default): a cell that is not seen keeps its votes unchanged, however long (hidden by a person or a car, out of view, behind the user). (3) **Confrontation** (`Fusion._add_confronted`, `conflictFactor` 0.4, `voteCap` 6.0): in a SEEN cell the votes of every other class of the same layer group (surface / object) are multiplied by `conflictFactor` before the new vote is added, and the group's total at the cell is capped at `voteCap` (two full-confidence weights), so a long history outvotes the present for one or two frames only. The negative evidence (`objectClearFactor`, rays, `surfaceHiddenFactor` behind buildings) stays. (4) **Nothing is inferred:** `gapCloseM` 0 (off); the cells a face writes are `faceThicknessM` 0.1 (one step beyond the base cell; 0.3 = v26.2.9); the occlusion behind a building keeps its own `hiddenThicknessM` 0.3. (5) **Pose confronted with the map** (`poseAlign`, `WorldMap._align`): before fusing, heading offsets +-`alignDeg` (2.0, `alignStepDeg` 0.5) and then shifts forward / sideways +-`alignShiftM` (0.3, `alignShiftStepM` 0.1) of the dead-reckoned pose are tried; score = the share of the frame's seen surface cells (every `alignStride`-th, up to 20 m) whose stored winning surface class, where the map has one with >= `alignMinVotes` (1.0), equals the observed class. Each frame proposes its best candidate when it beats the unshifted pose by `alignMinGain` (0.01; plan 0.03 lagged a heading that drifts 0.8 deg/s on Chest-center-0004) with at least `alignMinCells` (500) cells compared (and not by comparing fewer than 90 % of the unshifted cells), else a zero proposal; the correction applied is the median of the last `alignWindow` (6) proposals (noise changes sign from frame to frame, a drift does not), at most `alignMoveDeg` (0.5) and `alignShiftStepM` (0.1) per frame; the correction accumulates in the map's own pose (`WorldMap.align_th`, `pos_c`; the dead-reckoned position steps are turned by it); the collision pass's `_WorldMemory` is untouched. `decay: "time"`, `conflictFactor` 1, `voteCap` 0, `poseAlign` false, `gapCloseM` 0.5, `faceThicknessM` 0.3 together restore v26.2.9 exactly (`tests/worldtracks_fixtures.v2629_configs`, pinned on every fixture window by `tests/test_worldtracks.py`; `tools/worldmap_agreement.py` variant `old`).
- **Agreement (v26.2.10):** `WorldMap` records per frame `agree` = the share of the seen cells (any class) where the snapshot's class equals the frame's own observation, and `agreePrior` = the share of the seen surface cells that the map already had right before the frame was fused (nan under `alignMinCells`). `worldmap.npz` adds `alignDeg` / `alignM` (accumulated heading / position correction), `agree`, `agreePrior` (N,); `pose` is the map's own (corrected) pose. `GET .../worldmap/pose.json` adds `alignDeg`, `alignM`, `agree` (arrays, null where undefined; absent for maps built before v26.2.10). `tools/worldmap_agreement.py <runId>` rebuilds the map offline with config variants (`old`, `new`, and `new` with each of persistence / confrontation / facts / alignment switched back) and prints mean / p10 of both and ms per frame.
- **Metrics:** `tools/worldmap_metrics.py <runId>` (cost, wall straightness, re-observation spread, drift proxy, extrusion; `--set key=value` config overrides; v26.2.7 `--rays t1,t2,...`: the ray scan of the stored snapshots, per time the building cells, the *gap* rays (a ray from the user whose first face cell is preceded by unknown cells that follow a surface cell) and the *through* rays (surface cells within 30 cells after the first face run)); `tools/worldmap_render.py <runId> <outDir> <t>...` (snapshot next to the camera frame).
