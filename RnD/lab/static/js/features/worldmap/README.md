### 9 Frontend — world map panel

**World map panel** (v26.2 step 1 spike, `js/features/worldmap/worldmap.js`; `#secWorldmap` is always visible, right of the video in `#viewer` (v26.2.0; the legend is the canvas tooltip, `#wmInfo` / `#wmStatus` sit in a slim bar under the canvas; v26.2.6: no Build map button, the map is built by the analysis job)). Canvas `#wmCanvas` (square, fills its column, re-sized and redrawn at devicePixelRatio by a ResizeObserver) shows the fused snapshot of the frame at the playhead (§8.1; frame found by `pose.json` `t`, none outside the run range): cells coloured through the mask's class LUT (`overlay.style`, so colours, hidden classes and level edits follow the Classes panel; unknown cells stay background), the user marker (col 100 / row 200, the bottom edge), the ±0.6 m corridor (red box) to 10 steps, clock spokes from the marker (hours 9..3 to the canvas edge, quarter hours at 7.5° to 10 steps; 1 h = 30°, 12 = straight ahead, as `clock_quarter` / `clock_text`), hour labels at the outer spoke ends, faint rings at every step to 10 steps with tick marks where the quarter spokes cross them, range rings every 5 steps labelled `N st` (step = the frame's `ego.stepLengthM`, default 0.7 m), and the current hazards frame's items at `(lateralM, distanceM)` (dots coloured by role, movers = diamonds with a white outline, role danger = extra ring, role none dimmed; v26.2.2: a coasting mover is a hollow dashed diamond, an item snapped to a solid area has a dashed line from its raw position to the corrected one). v26.2.6: items of class building, structure bridge and structure tunnel get no marker (their cells and wall lines show them). Always drawn (v26.2.6; the `#wmRules` toggle is gone): the **wall line** (yellow, v26.2.7: on the building's edge, i.e. on every cell border between a face-class cell and a 4-neighbour surface cell of the snapshot shown, from `faceClassIds` / `surfaceClassIds`; the canvas carries the segment count in `data-wall-edges`; the fitted `faceEdgeLines` stay in `hazards.json` for the rule but are no longer drawn), face cells (magenta) and edge cells (cyan) of the walls judged on the map. `#wmInfo` shows the frame count and mean / p95 ms, or "This run has no world map." Every map request carries `?v=<builtAt>` of the `GET /worldmap` meta (v26.2.7; the PNGs are cached for a day and a re-run rebuilds the map under the same URL; the cell cache key includes it). Snapshots are fetched on every seek / playhead change when paused and at most 6 per second while playing (latest wins), decoded to class grids and kept in a 24-entry cache. **Click readout (v26.2.4; the hover readout `#wmReadout` is gone):** a click / tap on the canvas selects a cell (v26.2.6: a click within an item marker's drawn radius, at least 8 CSS px, selects that item instead, nearest wins, and snaps the selection to its cell): a yellow crosshair box marks it and stays while the frame changes, until another click. The text box `#wmCell` under the map (never overlaid; on a phone the map sits under the video and the box under the map) shows for the selected cell at the current frame: the clock, steps, metres ahead / to the side and the grid cell (col, row); `Region:` the surface layer with its safety level (Classes panel levels, default `safety_mapping.json`); `Object:` the selected item (class, role, track, distance source; followed by track id across frames while it stays on the cell), else the nearest drawn marker covering the cell, plus the object layer as `map: <class>`; with no item, the object layer; `Item:` every hazards item whose position (map / snapped / coasting position, the raw position of a snapped item, the image measurement) is within 0.3 m of the cell, with class, role, track id, distance source and `inside solid` / `coasting` / `ghost` marks (item footprints are not stored, so only positions are matched). A cell with no surface, no object and no item shows its coordinate line (clock, steps, metres, col / row) and then `unmapped` (v26.2.7). E2E: `test_worldmap.py` (map present on the session run without a click, no `#wmBuild` / `#wmRules`, no "Beyond 20 m", a click on an item marker names it on the Object line, snapshot request 200 and carrying `v=`, canvas non-empty, click on a mapped and on an unmapped cell (the unmapped one keeps its coordinate line), selection kept across frames, `faceClassIds` in the map meta, wall-edge segments drawn). **Items as a whole (v26.2.10; shared with the video, `js/util/selection.js`):** every drawn item is also drawn as its **footprint**: a 2 px segment across its width at its distance, from its bbox edges through the lens (rectilinear `tan b = (x - 0.5) * 2 tan(hfov / 2)`, fisheye `b = (x - 0.5) * hfov`; the lateral spread of an edge is `distanceM * (tan b_edge - tan b_centre)` around the item's own lateral position, clamped to 6 m; the lens comes from `settings.hfovDeg` and the hazards response's `projection`); movers keep their diamond, a coasting item its dashed line. A click within an item's marker or footprint (at least 8 CSS px) **selects that item** (the click behaves as before for the cell: crosshair, readout) and the same item (matched by `trackId` + class in the frame) is selected on the video; hovering shows the **tooltip** (`#itemTip`, shared with the video): `class · role <role> · track <id>`, `<m> m · <steps> steps · <clock>`, `distance source <source>`, and `world track <W id> · <speed> m/s` when the frame's `worldTracks[]` has an entry with the same `trackId` (not dropped). A selected item is ringed and its footprint thickened (yellow on black); `data-sel-track` on the canvas = its track id ('' = none; also on `#overlay`). A click on an **object-layer cell with no item** (the cell endpoint reports an object) selects the **whole 8-connected same-class region of the snapshot shown**: its outline (every border between a region cell and a cell that is not) is drawn yellow on black, `data-sel-region` = its cell count, re-grown from the seed cell on every snapshot (empty when the seed no longer has that class); hovering inside it shows `<class> region / N cells / nearest X m / clock a to b` (nearest = the closest region cell to the user; the clock range from the bearings of its cells). A click on nothing (or Esc, or loading another run / collision run) clears the selection. The readout `#wmCell` under the map stays and is **led by the selection**: `Selected: <the item's tooltip lines joined with ·>` or `Selected: <region lines>`, then the cell lines as before; with a selection made on the video and no cell clicked, the readout is the `Selected:` line alone. E2E hook: `window.__labWorldmap` (the panel). **World tracks (v26.2.9):** the frame's `worldTracks` are drawn under the item markers at (right, fwd): faint 1-σ ring, an arrow of 1 s of ground velocity (>= 4 px when faster than 0.2 m/s), solid when `seen`, dashed when `out-of-view`, grey dashed when `missing`, the danger colour plus a dotted line to the closest-approach point when `onCourse`; `data-world-tracks` on the canvas = their count. A click within a track's ring (or 8 CSS px) adds `World track W12 · person · seen · 1.3 m/s toward 2:00 · closest 0.3 m in 4.2 s (on course) · σ 0.4 m · images 17, 23`; the item marker keeps the `Object:` line. E2E: `test_world_tracks_are_in_the_hazards_drawn_and_read_on_click`.
